1 · Three arms

Kunzmann once · Galleri every year
{{FIG_ARMS}}

Kunzmann is scored once at the start age; {{KSHARE}} score 8 or more (GRADE evidence profile, sensitivity {{KCAP}}, specificity 64.4%) and get one EGD, then usual care. Galleri sensitivity is {{SENS_G}} in round 1 and ×{{REP}} after. Every end state feeds the natural-history model (figure 5).

2 · Galleri +ve: the EGD step

hold · two kinds of miss
{{FIG_EGD}}

After a negative EGD, repeat positives skip EGD for {{HOLD}} years (the shorter of the gastric 2.37-year and esophageal adenocarcinoma 4.9-year sojourn estimates) and go straight to ENT or PET CT. EGD finds {{SE}} of cancers present (Menon 2014). Of the misses, {{FI}} are cancers not yet visible (Kamran 2023 root causes A + C), which become visible after {{TVIS}} year; the rest are visible lesions that were missed.

3 · After a negative or skipped EGD

ENT or PET CT
{{FIG_REST}}

A missed visible upper GI cancer can be seen on PET CT (*{{PETUGI}}, a DUMMY placeholder) and is then found by a targeted EGD. Incidental findings are followed up; the benign ones count toward the false-positive burden.

4 · Pancreatobiliary finding, by history

tumor board · coin-toss sampling
{{FIG_PB1}} {{FIG_PB2}}

First finding: EUS, with FNA/FNB a coin toss (beta(2,2)); a negative or unsampled EUS goes to tumor board, which sends {{TL}} to diagnostic laparoscopy and the rest to watchful waiting. A repeat finding is a personalised decision: after a negative EUS, laparoscopy is half as likely ({{PL2}}) and repeat EUS {{PE2}}; after a negative laparoscopy, laparoscopy is a tenth as likely ({{PL3}}) and repeat EUS {{PE3}}. The history is permanent.

Repeat positives after a negative workup

Each person has their own false-positive propensity, drawn from beta(mκ, (1−m)κ) with mean m = {{FPR}} per test. After k earlier false positives in t − 1 tests, the chance the next test is falsely positive is (mκ + k) / (κ + t − 1). The base case uses κ = {{KAPPA}} (tests independent); the scenarios κ = 3,400 and 2,900 make a second false positive 1.8× and 1.96× as likely. The cohort model carries tags for 0, 1 and 2+ earlier false positives; the microsimulation draws each person's propensity.

5 · Natural history

same in every arm
{{FIG_NH}}

Onset is solved so that, without screening, symptomatic diagnoses reproduce SEER incidence by age, sex and race/ethnicity, and their stage reproduces SEER's stage mix. Screen-detected cancers wait in lead-time states until they would have caused symptoms, so screening earns stage shift only. The cohort model steps once a year; the microsimulation runs the same hazards in continuous time (hesim).

6 · Esophageal adenocarcinoma calibrated to MISCAN-ESO

scenario · cohort model
{{FIG_EAC}}

In the MISCAN-ESO scenario, esophageal adenocarcinoma (EAC) follows the CISNET MISCAN-ESO structure: preclinical cancer moves from an early to a late stage before symptoms, with exponential durations. Mean sojourn {{EAC_S_M}} years in men and {{EAC_S_F}} in women (Kong 2014). The three rates are solved so that the sojourn, SEER's late-stage share at diagnosis (net of other-cause death) and the early/late dwell ratio are met exactly. A screen-detected EAC is early if found in the early phase, so its stage comes from the natural history. Gastric and other esophageal cancer keep the base-case natural history. Diagnosed EAC has esophagus-specific survival.

Microsimulation: following each person

The same inputs run through a hesim individual-level model: 1,000,000 simulated people, each followed through all three arms with the same random draws, so every person's outcome under Galleri can be compared with their own outcome under usual care.

What changed since the last version

  • EGD hold of {{HOLD}} years after a negative EGD; repeat positives go straight to ENT or PET CT.
  • Two kinds of EGD miss: a visible lesion missed, and a cancer not yet visible.
  • Pancreatobiliary workup by history: coin-toss FNA/FNB, tumor board, laparoscopy less likely after each negative result.
  • Bayesian repeat positives (κ scenarios), and Kunzmann as a one-time test from the GRADE evidence profile.
  • Person-level microsimulation (hesim) that cross-checks the cohort model; it also corrected how workup paths are weighted for people with cancers outside the upper GI tract.
  • MISCAN-ESO calibration of esophageal adenocarcinoma (scenario): sex-specific sojourn, early → late preclinical progression, stage of screen-detected EAC from the natural history, esophagus-specific survival; validated against CISNET lifetime cases and deaths.
  • Reproducible in R only: the inputs workbook and all three pages are now built by R scripts; Rscript make_all.R reruns everything.

Still not modelled: stage varying with age within a race-sex group, the natural history of other-site cancers (they borrow upper GI's), and a hold that depends on a Bayesian probability threshold.
