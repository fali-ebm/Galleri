02 · THE WORKUP

A positive test starts a chain of procedures

Every positive gets an endoscopy (EGD). When it finds nothing, the search moves on: an ENT exam or a whole-body PET CT, then endoscopic ultrasound or a biopsy of whatever else lights up. Widths are men, lifetime, per 100,000.

05 · SAME TEST, DIFFERENT ODDS

A positive result means less for a woman in her fifties than for a man in his eighties

The test is equally sensitive and specific for everyone. What changes is how many people have cancer to find, which comes from SEER rates by age, sex and race. Each cell is one year of age: how many cancer-free people are worked up for every cancer the test finds.

06 · OVERDIAGNOSIS

The older the man, the more often the cancer found would never have mattered

Overdiagnosed: a cancer found by screening that would never have caused symptoms, because the man would have died of something else first. Bars are cancers found each year of age.
