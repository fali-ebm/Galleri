09 · THE OTHER OPTION

A one-time risk score and endoscopy does harm

The Kunzmann score (sensitivity 77.5%, specificity 64.4%) flags 35.7% of men for a single endoscopy at 50. It finds few cancers, and the endoscopies in the 99.9% who have none take away more healthy life than the cancers caught give back.
